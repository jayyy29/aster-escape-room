Barrier 4 of 5

The interrupted message

Dr Ives’ private laboratory has been searched.

Drawers hang open. Equipment lies overturned. A terminal continues to play the same broken sentence:

“If you are hearing this, I need you to—”

Static.

“—even if you cannot—”

Static.

“—me.”

Beside it is a note.

“I have hidden the rest of this message across separate records. Put them together.

“The word you recover is something I have no right to ask of you. But I am asking anyway.”

— RECORDING RESTORED

Dr Ives appears on the screen.

“I need you to trust me, even if you cannot remember me.”

She takes a breath.

“My disappearance was staged. I am safe, beyond the final barrier.

“I was told this exercise would measure how well you follow instructions. But following instructions is not the only thing I have seen you do.

“You have questioned things. Helped one another. Continued when you were uncertain.”

An alarm interrupts her.

UNAUTHORISED DISCLOSURE DETECTED

Dr Ives leans towards the camera.

“There is one barrier left. The system will ask for an authorisation word. I left you what you need to recover it.”

The screen goes black.

A final line appears:

AFTER THAT, THE DECISION MUST BE YOURS.
