The scientist’s desk

Dr Ives’ office looks as though she stepped out only moments ago.

An open notebook lies beside a locked desk drawer. Most of its pages have been torn out. On the remaining page, someone has written:

WHEN THEY SAY I DISAPPEARED, THAT IS WHEN YOUR SEARCH BEGINS.

The drawer requires a four-digit code.

Recover the code from the evidence on her desk.

— ACCESS GRANTED

Inside the drawer is a security pass and a handwritten note.

“I could not risk leaving you a direct route. The system reads everything I write.

“Find the place where we keep the things we cannot afford to forget.”

Beneath the note is a photograph of Dr Ives standing beside several people. Their faces have been carefully cut out.

You turn it over.

OUR FIRST DAY TOGETHER.

You do not remember meeting her.

Somehow, her handwriting feels familiar.
