The memory archive

The security pass opens the archive.

Rows of storage cabinets stretch into the darkness. Each is marked with a different sector name. A directory screen glows beside the entrance, but Dr Ives’ records have been removed from the search results.

A paper strip has been tucked beneath the keyboard.

“An echo is something that returns after its source has gone.

“If any part of our work survives, you will find it there.”

Identify the correct archive sector.

— SECTOR UNLOCKED

A recording opens.

Dr Ives sits at a laboratory table.

“Session forty-three. An unexpected development today. During a routine exercise, one participant stopped working to help another.”

An unseen voice asks, “Was cooperation part of the instructions?”

“No.”

“Was there a reward?”

“No.”

Dr Ives pauses.

“They knew it would cost them time.”

The recording ends.

A floor plan appears, with a route leading towards an observation gallery.

For a moment, another message flashes across the screen:

PREVIOUS SESSION RECORDS UNAVAILABLE

Then it vanishes.
