Barrier 3 of 5

The observation gallery

Beyond the archive lies a gallery lined with observation windows.

On the other side of each window is an empty room.

One contains a desk arranged exactly like Dr Ives’.

Another contains an archive cabinet labelled ECHO.

A third is dark.

Beneath the glass, someone has scratched five words:

No One Reaches Truth Here.

A damaged facility map lies on the control panel. Several corridors have been sealed, but one route still leads to Dr Ives’ private laboratory.

Determine which direction to take.

— CORRIDOR ACCESS RESTORED

As you leave, the dark observation room lights up.

Inside is an exact copy of the room where your search began.

The same screen. The same furniture.

Even the same damaged recording.

A security monitor switches on beside you. It shows your team standing in the gallery.

Above the image is a label:

GROUP 43 — RESPONSE WITHIN EXPECTED RANGE

The label disappears.

The facility’s automated voice speaks.

“Please continue towards Dr Ives. Unnecessary investigation will reduce the time available.”

Ahead, the door to her private laboratory opens.
